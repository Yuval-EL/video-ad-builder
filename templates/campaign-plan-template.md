# Campaign Plan: <ad name>

One page. Written by the builder after the script is approved, updated when the kit is finished. Plain prose. This is the document a marketing lead reads first.

## Goal
One sentence. What the campaign is for and who it is for.

## Done when
One measurable bar, with a number and a time box. Example shape: "Done when one hook leads the other two on view-through past five seconds by a clear margin after a fixed test budget, and the winner is running in the sequence below."

## Step 1. Hook test
- Files: the three masters in `master/`.
- Setup: same audience, same budget, same placement. Only the first five seconds differ.
- What each hook is betting on, one line each, with its pattern ID.
- Decision rule: which number decides, and what counts as a clear margin.
- Fallback if no hook wins clearly.

## Step 2. Sequence
| Order | File | Shown to | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 second bumper | Everyone in the audience | Plant the one number |
| 2 | 30 second winner | People who saw the bumper | Tell the story |
| 3 | 15 second cutdown | People who watched most of the 30 | Remind and ask |

## Where each file goes
| File | Placement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 16:9 masters | YouTube in-stream, desktop and TV | Skippable. Brand and promise before second five. |
| 9:16 | YouTube Shorts and mobile | Safe area respected. |
| 1:1 at 25 fps | LinkedIn feed, other feeds | Upload captions. Most viewers are muted. |
| Captions, thumbnail, ad text | Upload fields | Lengths already checked against platform limits. |

## Reaching this audience
Filled from the audience research when it exists. Otherwise marked "needs research."
- YouTube: custom segments from search terms the buyer uses, in-market segments for business software, customer lists if available.
- LinkedIn: job titles, company size, industries.
- Exclusions.

## What to measure
Three numbers, not twenty.
1. View-through past five seconds, per hook.
2. Completed views of the 30 second ad.
3. The business action, for example demo requests per thousand completed views.

## Claims check
Every number and named integration in the ad, with its source. Anything illustrative is marked.

## Open items
Music license, missing screenshots, approvals still needed.
