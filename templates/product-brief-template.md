# Product Brief: <capability name>

One file per capability the ads may feature. Lives in `products/<name>/product-knowledge/`.

## What it does
Two or three sentences in plain language. What the user does, what the product does in response, what the user gets.

## Who it is for
The role that cares. One line.

## The moment it matters
The situation in which this capability earns its keep. One short scene, written like a story beat.

## Proof
Any real number, customer, or integration that backs it. Mark anything illustrative.

## Visual anchor
If a screenshot exists: filename and the zone to crop to.
If not: which illustrated card type fits (conversation, workflow, pills, metrics, big text).

## Words to use
Five to ten phrases the buyer would use for this. Pull from audience research when available.

## Words to avoid
Internal jargon, feature names the buyer would not recognize.
