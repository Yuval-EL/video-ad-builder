# LinkedIn Video Ad Specs

Pulled from LinkedIn's own help page on 2026-09-17.

## Source
- Video ads advertising specifications, Marketing Solutions Help: https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a424737

## Resolution and aspect ratio
| Orientation | Ratio | Recommended | Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landscape | 16:9 | 1920 x 1080 | 640 x 360 to 1920 x 1080 |
| Square | 1:1 | 1080 x 1080 | 360 x 360 to 1920 x 1920 |
| Vertical | 4:5 | 720 x 900 | 360 x 450 to 1080 x 1350 |
| Vertical | 9:16 | 720 x 1280 | 360 x 640 to 1080 x 1920 |

## Encoding
- File type: MP4.
- File size: 75 KB to 500 MB.
- Frame rate: less than 30 frames per second. **The builder renders LinkedIn files at 25.**
- Audio: AAC.
- Captions: SRT file, uploaded separately.

## Duration
- Allowed: 3 seconds to 30 minutes.
- LinkedIn recommends 15 to 30 seconds.

## Text
- Headline: up to 70 characters shows without truncation.
- Introductory text: up to 150 characters shows without truncation.

## Craft consequences
- The feed is muted by default. Upload the caption file every time. For LinkedIn-first ads, prefer the Silent Scroller playbook or burn captions in on request.
- Square holds the most feed space on both desktop and mobile. It is the default LinkedIn format in the kit.
- The 59 second length fits here better than on YouTube.
