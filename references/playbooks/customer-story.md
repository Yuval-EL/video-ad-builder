# Playbook: Customer Story

A real person talks about how they use the product, intercut with the product itself.

## When to use
- Trust-building campaigns where credibility matters more than feature density.
- Partner or integration stories.
- When the product needs to feel human.

## Requirements
This playbook needs real footage of a real customer. Without it, do not simulate a person. Fall back to Narrated Demo with a text-based quote card, or ask the user for footage.

## Core DNA (always true)
1. Real people speaking in first person, not third-person narration.
2. Interface shots interleaved with the person. Never more than two consecutive shots of either.
3. One hard number anchors the story.
4. Stock footage is forbidden. It kills authenticity instantly.
5. Music ducked under speech at about 18 percent.

## Default patterns
Hook A6 or A3. Structure B1 with the proof act carried by the person. Color C5. Composition D9 with D1 or D6 for the interface beats. Motion E1, E6, E10. Voice G2 in the person's own words, with the builder only trimming. Action H2 with H3, or H1. Trust I6 and I1.

## Craft rules
- Sound bites, not monologues. Short clips cut against product visuals.
- Natural speech rhythm. Slightly imperfect sounds more honest than studio-clean.
- Lower thirds with name, title, and company on the first appearance.

## Done when
- The person appears within the first four seconds.
- One number gets its own full-screen beat.
- No two consecutive shots are of the same kind.
