# Playbook: Silent Scroller

No voice. Text and motion carry everything. Built for people watching with the sound off.

## When to use
- Feeds where most viewers are muted: social stories, reels, in-feed placements.
- Brand awareness or product announcements.
- When production speed matters, because there is no voiceover cycle.
- When the same file must work with and without sound.

## Core DNA (always true)
1. The ad is fully understandable on mute. Sound is a bonus.
2. Text carries all messaging. One headline per scene, five to seven words, large enough for a phone.
3. Music only, as a mood layer.
4. Motion replaces voice as the attention driver: typewriter text, animated cursor, progressive builds.
5. One idea per screen. Reading costs more attention than listening.

## Default patterns
Hook A2, A3, or A7. Structure B4 or B6 for 15 seconds, B1 for 30 seconds. Color C2 or C3 with C6 on every text card. Composition D7, D5, D4, D8. Motion E5, E2, E7, E10. No voice. Action H1 or H2. Trust I7 or I8.

## Typography rules
- Text is the message. Bold, high contrast, readable at arm's length.
- One keyword in the accent color per card.
- Typewriter animation for the key phrases.
- Massive type on the closer, held still.

## Done when
- Every scene makes sense with the audio removed.
- No text card exceeds seven words.
- Each card holds long enough to read twice.
- The closer is the largest type in the ad.
